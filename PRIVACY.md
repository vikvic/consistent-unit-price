# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Consistent Unit Price does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

## Information processed locally

The extension reads visible product and unit-price text on supported Amazon.com and Walmart.com pages so it can identify explicit weight-based unit prices and display them in the format selected by the user. This processing happens entirely inside the browser.

The extension stores the following preferences locally through the browser's extension storage:

- Preferred unit-price format.
- Manual or automatic format selection.
- Display style.
- Whether Amazon and Walmart support is enabled.

These preferences are not transmitted outside the browser.

## Information not collected

Consistent Unit Price does not collect or transmit:

- Browsing history.
- Product or pricing data.
- Page contents.
- Account credentials.
- Personal or financial information.
- Analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or crash reports.

The extension does not use a remote server, analytics provider, advertising service, account system, or AI service.

## Permissions

The `storage` permission saves extension preferences locally. Access to Amazon.com and Walmart.com is used only to recognize and restyle explicit weight-based unit-price labels on those pages.

## Changes

If the extension's data practices change, this policy will be updated before a release containing those changes is published.
